About this prototype

The problem it tackles

People learn a word faster when they use it, not only when they hear it. A voice agent can run that loop without a teacher: teach, prompt, listen, respond.

How a session works

  • Five words per session. The agent says the word, its meaning and an example.
  • You use the word in a sentence, spoken or typed. You get two tries.
  • Missed words come back once at the end of the session.

What I would measure

  • Session completion rate and drop-off by word
  • Share of sentences that use the target word on the first try
  • Retry rate and how many revisited words are passed
  • Whether users return the next day

What it cannot do yet

  • It checks that you used the word in a full sentence of five or more words. It does not judge whether the meaning is right. The next version would add an LLM check for meaning and tone.
  • Word list is fixed. Next: level selection and spaced repetition.
  • Speech input needs Chrome or Edge. Typing works everywhere.

Built with Claude as plain HTML, using the browser's speech tools. No data leaves your device.
